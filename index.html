About Game

What is Forza Horizon 6?

Forza Horizon 6 is an open-world racing game from Playground Games set in a dense recreation of Japan. Players drive across Tokyo, rural roads, mountain passes, industrial zones, and festival routes in hundreds of real-world cars.

The game focuses on exploration, car collecting, tuning, seasonal Festival Playlist rewards, multiplayer events, EventLab creations, and a progression path from tourist to Horizon Legend.
