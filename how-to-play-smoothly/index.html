Smooth Gameplay Guide

Forza Horizon 6: How to Play It Smoothly

Players searching how to play Forza Horizon 6 smoothly usually need a simple answer: reduce VRAM pressure, use upscaling carefully, avoid expensive ray tracing first, and test changes on the same road route. This guide turns the PC settings page into a quick action plan.

Quick Answer

For most mid-range PCs, start with High textures, High geometry, High screen-space reflections, Medium or High screen-space GI, ray-traced reflections off, RTGI off or Medium only on stronger GPUs, and upscaling on Quality or Balanced at higher resolutions.

Use One Test Route

Pick one route with traffic, reflections and fast corners, then repeat it after each settings change. Smoothness is easier to feel when you stop changing the car, weather and route at the same time.

Watch VRAM Before Visuals

If an 8GB GPU stutters, do not start by lowering everything. Keep the settings that preserve driving clarity, then reduce Extreme textures, heavy RT features and particle effects first.

Use the Settings Recommender

The PC Settings page has an interactive recommender that outputs a preset based on VRAM, resolution, FPS target and ray tracing preference.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to make Forza Horizon 6 smoother?

Turn off ray-traced reflections first, use High instead of Extreme textures on 8GB GPUs, and test upscaling Quality or Balanced.

Why does the game stutter even when average FPS is high?

Average FPS can hide VRAM spikes, shader compilation, overlays, background capture tools or slow storage.

Should I use DLSS, FSR or XeSS?

Use the upscaler that looks cleanest on your GPU and monitor. Compare moving cars and fences, not only static screenshots.

Is the PC Settings page more detailed?

Yes. Use /pc-settings/ for a full preset table and interactive recommender.
